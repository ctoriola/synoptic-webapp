Frequently Asked Questions

How do tokens work?

Each AI proposal generation uses 1 token. Tokens reset monthly with your subscription. Unused tokens don't roll over to the next month.

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Yes! You can change your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, while downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

What happens if I run out of tokens?

You'll need to wait until your next billing cycle or upgrade your plan to get more tokens immediately.
